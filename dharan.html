EAT LIKE A LOCAL

Come hungry.

Dharan's food scene is one of the best reasons to spend an evening in the city.

🔥 Sekuwa

Charcoal-grilled meat is one of Dharan's signature food experiences. Bagarkot is particularly associated with Dharane-style sekuwa and Rai/Limbu food culture.

DON'T LEAVE WITHOUT TRYING

🥟 Mo:Mo

From simple local shops to busy evening food spots, momo is an easy first choice. Try a small plate before committing to a huge food crawl.

EASY + AFFORDABLE

🥩 Local Pork Dishes

Dharan has a strong reputation for pork dishes and local Rai/Limbu-influenced flavours. Ask what the house specialty is instead of ordering blindly.

LOCAL FLAVOUR

🌶️ Aalu Nimki & Street Snacks

If you are exploring in the evening, leave room for smaller snacks. Street food is part of the fun — especially around busy market areas.
