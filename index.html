🔥 Sekuwa

Charcoal-grilled meat is one of Dharan's signature food experiences. Bagarkot is particularly associated with Dharane-style sekuwa and Rai/Limbu food culture.

FIRST VISIT GUIDE

Start here.

Practical tips to make your first trip to Dharan effortless.

🚗 Getting Around

Auto Rickshaws (Tuk-tuks) and local Safa Tempos are the primary way to move inside town. Always agree on the fare before hopping in if non-metered, or use local ride apps if available.

☀️ Best Time to Explore

Head to the Vijayapur Hill temples in the cooler morning hours. Save the late afternoon for Bhanu Chowk market walks and evening sekuwa in Bagarkot.
